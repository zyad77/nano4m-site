CLEVR fidelity & CLIP on 200 val samples. V1 wins — CLEVR 0.4735 (+0.0509 over baseline). All variants beat baseline on fidelity.

Interpretation & Discussion
No universal best strategy. Structure helps when aligned with the generation direction. Randomness remains useful for diversity.
Key takeaway: Structure is useful, but the best results come from balancing modality-aware masking with enough randomness to keep the task diverse. The optimal policy is direction-dependent — not universal.
