Final evaluation loss

Final Evaluation Loss by Masking Strategy

Final eval loss at step 38,140. Lower is better. These are evaluation loss values — not training loss.

Baseline and V1 (span masking) achieve the lowest final eval loss (~3.53), nearly identical. V4 sits at ~3.85 due to its mixed objective. V2 and V3 plateau highest (~4.15) — block masking creates harder reconstruction tasks, not worse models. Lower eval loss does not directly predict generation quality.
