What the Report Covers

Six sections covering the full research pipeline from motivation to individual contributions.

I Introduction
Motivation
Why random masking is suboptimal; how structure in text and image modalities motivates alternative masking policies.
II Related Work
Literature Context
BERT, SpanBERT, T5, MAE, BEiT, MultiMAE — positioning our work in the masked modeling landscape.
III Method
Masking Primitives
Span, block, and mixed masking implementations with Dirichlet budget allocation and sentinel tokens.
IV Experiments
Results & Analysis
Training loss, image-to-text (BLEU/CIDEr/ROUGE), text-to-image (CLEVR fidelity + CLIP) across 5 variants.
V Conclusion
Takeaways & Limitations
Direction-dependent optimality, single-dataset limitations, small-scale caveats, and future directions.
VI Contributions
Individual Contributions
Detailed breakdown of what each team member implemented, tested, and wrote.
